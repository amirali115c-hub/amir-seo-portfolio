THE PROBLEM
A Site Google Couldn't Trust

Carbon Reprographics operated three e-commerce verticals: jewelry, automotive, and home decor. The site had serious technical SEO issues holding it back. Broken internal links were everywhere, duplicate content was dragging down crawl efficiency, and product pages had no structured data.

The internal linking was flat with no hierarchy, meaning PageRank was not flowing to the pages that mattered most. On-page optimization was weak across the board with thin meta descriptions and poorly structured headings.

THE APPROACH
A Four-Phase Technical Overhaul
Phase 01

Full Technical Audit with Screaming Frog

Ran a comprehensive Screaming Frog crawl and identified 340+ broken internal links, 89 duplicate content issues, missing canonical tags, and parameter URLs wasting crawl budget. Every issue was logged with severity ranking and fixed systematically.

Phase 02

Internal Linking Architecture Rebuild

Built a proper internal linking hierarchy connecting product categories, subcategories, and related products. Implemented breadcrumb navigation with structured data. Created hub pages for each vertical that distributed PageRank to product pages efficiently.

Phase 03

On-Page Optimization at Scale

Rewrote 85 product descriptions with benefit-driven copy. Added optimized meta titles, meta descriptions, and proper H1/H2 heading hierarchy to every product and category page. Ensured each page targeted a specific keyword without cannibalization.

Phase 04

Structured Data and Schema Implementation

Implemented JSON-LD product schema across all product pages. Added breadcrumb schema, organization schema, and FAQ schema where applicable. This improved how Google understood the site structure and enhanced search appearance.
