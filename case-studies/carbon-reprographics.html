The Challenge

Carbon Reprographics operated across three e-commerce verticals — jewelry, automotive, and home decor — with over 200 product pages and blog articles. Most content was thin, poorly optimized, and competing in high-competition niches against established brands. Organic traffic was flat at 12K monthly visitors, and product pages weren't ranking for buyer-intent keywords.

The Strategy

1. Technical Audit

Ran full Screaming Frog crawl to identify 340+ broken internal links, 89 duplicate content issues, and crawl budget waste from parameter URLs. Fixed all crawl errors and implemented canonical tags.

2. Keyword Mapping

Built keyword maps for every product category using Ahrefs and SEMrush. Mapped buyer-intent keywords to product pages and informational queries to blog content. Identified 150+ untapped keyword opportunities.

3. Content Restructuring

Rewrote 85 product descriptions from feature-focused to benefit-driven copy. Added optimized meta titles, descriptions, and H1/H2 hierarchy. Implemented internal linking strategy connecting related products.

4. Content Expansion

Created 120 SEO-optimized blog articles targeting long-tail keywords in each vertical. Built topic clusters around core product categories to establish topical authority.

What I Did

  • Optimized 120 blog articles, product pages, and category descriptions across three verticals
  • Restructured 85 product descriptions into benefit-driven narratives with optimized meta tags
  • Fixed 340+ broken internal links and resolved duplicate content issues
  • Implemented strategic internal linking across product clusters
  • Built 120 new SEO articles targeting buyer-intent long-tail keywords
  • Applied JSON-LD structured data for product schema across all product pages
  • Set up GA4 tracking and GSC monitoring for all three categories

The Results

Within 12 months, organic traffic grew from 12K to 180K monthly visitors — a 15x increase. Product pages started ranking on Page 1 for competitive buyer-intent keywords. The internal linking strategy reduced bounce rate by 28% and increased average session duration by 45%. Conversion rates improved by 34% across all three verticals.
