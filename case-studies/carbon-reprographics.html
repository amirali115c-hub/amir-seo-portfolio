The Problem

Carbon Reprographics ran three e-commerce verticals: jewelry, automotive, and home decor. The site had over 200 product pages and blog articles, but most of the content was thin, poorly optimized, and invisible on Google. Organic traffic sat flat at 12K monthly visitors for months. Product pages were competing in high-competition niches against established brands with years of domain authority. The site had no internal linking strategy, no structured data, and no content clusters.

The Approach

PHASE 01

Technical Audit and Crawl Cleanup

Ran a full Screaming Frog crawl and uncovered 340+ broken internal links, 89 duplicate content issues, and significant crawl budget waste from parameter URLs. Every broken link was fixed. Canonical tags were implemented across all duplicate pages. Crawl efficiency improved by 40% within the first two weeks.

PHASE 02

Keyword Architecture and Intent Mapping

Built comprehensive keyword maps for every product category using Ahrefs and SEMrush. Mapped buyer-intent keywords directly to product pages and informational queries to blog content. Identified 150+ untapped keyword opportunities across all three verticals that competitors were ranking for but Carbon Reprographics was ignoring.

PHASE 03

Content Restructuring and Optimization

Rewrote 85 product descriptions from feature-focused copy into benefit-driven narratives. Added optimized meta titles, descriptions, and proper H1/H2 heading hierarchy to every product and category page. Implemented a strategic internal linking structure connecting related products across all three verticals.

PHASE 04

Content Expansion and Topical Authority

Created 120 SEO-optimized blog articles targeting long-tail keywords in jewelry, automotive, and home decor. Built topic clusters around core product categories to establish topical authority. Each article was designed to capture informational search traffic and funnel visitors toward product pages.

The Outcome

Within 12 months, organic traffic grew from 12K to 180K monthly visitors. The 15x increase was driven by a combination of technical fixes, content restructuring, and strategic content expansion.
