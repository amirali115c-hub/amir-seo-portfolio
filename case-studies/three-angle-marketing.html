The Problem

Three Angle Marketing managed SEO for real estate and tourism clients in Dubai, two of the most competitive search verticals online. Their clients were competing against massive property portals and travel platforms with enormous budgets and years of content. The existing content was generic, not location-optimized, and completely failing to capture international search intent from buyers and travelers outside the UAE. Organic traffic was negligible. The agency needed a systematic approach to build organic visibility for their clients in a market where every keyword was contested.

The Approach

PHASE 01

Search Intent and Competitor Analysis

Analyzed search intent for Dubai real estate and tourism queries across 15+ countries. Identified specific gaps where international searchers were looking for location-based information that competitors were not providing. Built a keyword universe of 500+ target terms organized by intent, competition level, and client relevance.

PHASE 02

Location-Specific Content Production

Created 150 location-specific SEO articles and landing pages targeting Dubai neighborhoods, property types, tourist attractions, and travel experiences. Each piece was structured for direct answer extraction by Google and AI search engines, with entity-first content architecture ensuring search engines could clearly understand location entities.

PHASE 03

Entity Optimization and Structured Data

Built entity-first content architecture ensuring Google could clearly understand location entities, property types, and tourism categories. Implemented structured data for real estate listings and tourism content. Optimized heading hierarchies and meta content for location-based keyword clusters across all client accounts.

PHASE 04

Ongoing Optimization and Performance Tracking

Ran monthly competitor gap analyses to identify new ranking opportunities. Built tracking spreadsheets to monitor organic visibility across target keywords. Adjusted content strategy based on performance data, doubling down on what worked and pivoting away from what did not.
