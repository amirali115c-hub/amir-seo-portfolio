The Problem

Three Angle Marketing managed SEO for real estate and tourism clients in Dubai. The websites had generic content that was not location-optimized. Pages were competing against massive property portals with huge budgets. Technical SEO was inconsistent across client sites with duplicate content, weak heading hierarchies, and no internal linking strategy connecting related location pages. The agency needed a systematic technical approach to improve search visibility for their clients.

The Approach

PHASE 01

Technical Audit Across Client Sites

Ran Screaming Frog audits across all client websites to identify duplicate content, missing meta tags, broken links, and heading hierarchy issues. Created a unified technical fix log with severity rankings and implementation priorities for each client.

PHASE 02

Location Page Architecture and On-Page SEO

Created 150 location-specific pages targeting Dubai neighborhoods, property types, and tourist attractions. Each page was optimized with proper heading hierarchy, meta titles, meta descriptions, and entity-first content structure for direct answer extraction.

PHASE 03

Internal Linking for Location Clusters

Built internal linking structures connecting related location pages, neighborhood guides, and property type content. Created hub pages for each real estate area and tourism category that distributed link authority to deeper location pages.

PHASE 04

Content Scoring and Optimization

Implemented content scoring system to evaluate each page against competitors. Adjusted content depth, keyword density, and entity coverage based on scoring. Maintained tracking spreadsheets to monitor ranking movements and on-page improvements.
