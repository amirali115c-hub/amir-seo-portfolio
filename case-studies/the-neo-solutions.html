The Challenge

The Neo Solutions was a B2B consultancy with virtually zero online presence. No organic traffic, no content strategy, and no search visibility. They were competing against established consultancies with years of content积累. The goal was simple but ambitious — build authority from scratch and start generating enterprise inquiries through organic search.

The Strategy

1. Authority Building

Identified the consultancy's core service areas and built a content strategy around thought leadership. Created topical clusters covering industry pain points, case study frameworks, and service-page SEO.

2. Content Production

Wrote 80 thought leadership articles and service pages. Each piece optimized for specific B2B search queries with proper internal linking structure connecting related topics and services.

3. Link Acquisition

Produced 35 outreach-ready guest articles published on third-party industry platforms. Each article included contextual backlinks to build domain authority and referral traffic.

4. On-Page Optimization

Audited and optimized existing web pages through keyword integration and heading hierarchy improvements. Implemented structured data and improved page speed for better crawlability.

What I Did

  • Wrote 80 thought leadership articles and service pages targeting B2B consultancy keywords
  • Built topic clusters around core service areas to establish topical authority
  • Produced 35 guest articles for third-party industry platforms to acquire quality backlinks
  • Optimized existing web pages with strategic keyword integration and heading hierarchy
  • Implemented internal linking strategy connecting related content and service pages
  • Set up GA4 and GSC tracking to monitor organic performance and lead generation
  • Created editorial calendar ensuring consistent publishing cadence over 10 months

The Results

Within 10 months, the website grew from zero to 15K monthly organic visitors. Domain authority increased 3.2x through consistent guest posting and internal linking. The thought leadership content started generating enterprise inquiries directly from organic search. Service pages achieved Page 1 rankings for 12 high-intent B2B keywords, and the brand established itself as a credible voice in the consultancy space.
