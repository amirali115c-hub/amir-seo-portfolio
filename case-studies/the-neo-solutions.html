The Problem

The Neo Solutions built modular lending platforms and provided risk advisory for regulated financial institutions. Their website had no SEO foundation. No technical audit had been done, no internal linking strategy existed, and service pages were not optimized for search. The product hierarchy was complex with multiple lending platforms and advisory services, but the site architecture did not reflect this complexity. They needed a complete technical and on-page SEO buildout from zero.

The Approach

PHASE 01

Technical Foundation and Site Architecture

Ran a full technical audit and identified missing sitemaps, no structured data, thin meta descriptions, and heading hierarchy issues across all pages. Built a proper site architecture that reflected the product hierarchy: loan origination, loan management, decision management, and collection management systems.

PHASE 02

Service Page On-Page Optimization

Optimized 80 pages including service pages, product pages, blog articles, and landing pages. Each page received proper meta titles, meta descriptions, heading hierarchy, and keyword targeting. Content was structured to target specific B2B FinTech search queries with clear search intent alignment.

PHASE 03

Internal Linking for Complex Product Hierarchy

Built internal linking structures connecting related products, services, and advisory pages. Created hub pages for each product category that linked to detailed feature pages. Connected blog content to relevant service pages to distribute authority and improve crawl depth across the site.

PHASE 04

Structured Data and Schema Implementation

Implemented JSON-LD structured data for organization, product, and FAQ schema. Added breadcrumb navigation with structured data to improve site understanding. Optimized page speed and ensured proper indexing through Google Search Console configuration.
