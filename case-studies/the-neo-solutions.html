The Problem

The Neo Solutions built modular lending platforms and provided risk advisory for regulated financial institutions. They operated in a niche where trust and authority are everything. But their website had zero organic traffic, no content strategy, and no search visibility. They were invisible to the exact enterprise clients who needed their products. The Approach

PHASE 01

Content Strategy and Topic Architecture

Identified the consultancy's core service areas: loan origination systems, risk advisory, compliance, and digital transformation. Built a content strategy around thought leadership in these areas. Created topic clusters covering industry pain points, regulatory frameworks, and solution-oriented content that enterprise decision-makers actually search for.

PHASE 02

Thought Leadership Content Production

Wrote 80 thought leadership articles and service pages. Each piece targeted specific B2B search queries with proper internal linking structure connecting related topics and services. Content was written to establish The Neo Solutions as a credible voice in the FinTech and lending space, not just a service provider.

PHASE 03

Link Acquisition Through Guest Publishing

Produced 35 outreach-ready guest articles published on third-party industry platforms. Each article included contextual backlinks to build domain authority and referral traffic. Focused on publishing in FinTech, banking, and digital transformation publications where their target audience already consumed content.

PHASE 04

On-Page Optimization and Technical Foundation

Audited and optimized existing web pages through keyword integration and heading hierarchy improvements. Implemented structured data and improved page speed for better crawlability. Set up Google Analytics and Search Console tracking to monitor organic performance and lead generation from day one.
