HOW I WORK
From Topic to Published Page

A repeatable, human-led workflow that produces rankable content at scale. Every step has a quality gate.

Step 01

Research

SERP analysis, competitor gaps, keyword clusters, and audience intent gathered from live search data.

Step 02

Brief

A strategic brief: target keywords, unique angle, required examples, tone, and conversion goal.

Step 03

AI Draft

Structured prompts produce a fast, editorial-ready first draft from the approved brief.

Step 04

Humanize

AI fingerprints removed, voice injected, examples added, every claim fact-checked.

Step 05

SEO Optimize

Title, meta, headings, internal links, and schema optimized to rank and earn AI citations.

Step 06

Publish & Iterate

Published, monitored for rankings, AI visibility, and decay, then refreshed on a schedule.
